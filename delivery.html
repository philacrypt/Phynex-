Delivery Information

Everything you need to know about delivery options, delivery times, locations and what to expect after placing your order.

Delivery options

PHYNEX works with delivery partners to get your order safely to the address provided during checkout.

Delivery option Estimated time Availability
Standard Delivery Depending on your location Selected locations
Express Delivery Depending on your location Where available
Delivery times may vary depending on your location, product availability, weekends, public holidays and other circumstances affecting delivery.

How delivery works

After you place an order, the seller prepares your item for dispatch. Once the package is handed to the delivery partner, it is transported to the delivery address you provided.

  • Place your order through PHYNEX.
  • Your order is confirmed and prepared.
  • The package is handed to a delivery partner.
  • The package is delivered to your selected address.

Where we deliver

Delivery availability depends on the product and the location entered during checkout.

Before completing your order, check that your delivery address and contact information are correct.

Track your order

If tracking is available for your order, your tracking information will be provided after the order has been dispatched.

Important delivery information

  • Make sure your delivery address is complete and accurate.
  • Keep your phone number available in case the delivery partner needs to contact you.
  • Delivery times are estimates and may change because of circumstances outside PHYNEX's control.
  • Some products may have different delivery options depending on the seller and location.

Need help with a delivery?

If your order has been delayed or you need assistance with delivery, please contact PHYNEX support.
